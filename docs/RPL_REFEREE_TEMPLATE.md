# Referee / Third-Party Confirmation Template - ICT40120

Use this template for a supervisor, client, moderator, or other relevant third party who has seen your work.

## What this is for

This letter is not a character reference. It is a practical confirmation that the person has seen your work and can verify the scope, quality, and continuity of your ICT work.

## Template

[Date]

To whom it may concern,

I am writing to confirm that I know [Candidate Full Name] and have observed their work in relation to ICT/web development projects.

I have seen or reviewed work related to:
- Maestro AI
- Artist Pages / Supabase
- LRRecords public website and related published web content
- GitHub-based project history and documentation

In my view, [Candidate First Name] has demonstrated practical competence in areas such as:
- web application development and maintenance
- documentation and release support
- deployment and hosting awareness
- database / cloud integration work
- testing, debugging, and iterative improvement
