# RPL Submission Bundle Outline - ICT40120

Use this as the final packaging order when sending your RPL application to Upskilled.

## Suggested Submission Order

1. `01_RPL_Cover_Letter.pdf`
- Export from [docs/RPL_COVER_LETTER_DRAFT.md](RPL_COVER_LETTER_DRAFT.md).
- Purpose: frames your application, explains why Maestro and Artist Pages are relevant, and sets the assessor context.

2. `02_RPL_Evidence_Index.pdf`
- Export from [docs/RPL_ICT40120_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md](RPL_ICT40120_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md).
- Purpose: unit-to-evidence mapping, checklist, and public corroboration references.

3. `03_RPL_Maestro_Overview.pdf`
- Export from [docs/RPL_ICT40120_MAESTRO_OVERVIEW.md](RPL_ICT40120_MAESTRO_OVERVIEW.md).
- Purpose: primary project overview and detailed competence narrative.

4. `04_RPL_Screenshot_Appendix.pdf`
- Export from [docs/RPL_SCREENSHOT_APPENDIX_TEMPLATE.md](RPL_SCREENSHOT_APPENDIX_TEMPLATE.md) after adding your screenshots and short captions.
- Purpose: visual proof of real work, public continuity, GitHub profile, and representative repositories.

5. `05_RPL_Referee_Confirmation.pdf`
- Export or copy from [docs/RPL_REFEREE_TEMPLATE.md](RPL_REFEREE_TEMPLATE.md).
- Purpose: third-party confirmation that someone has seen your work and can verify scope and continuity.

6. `06_RPL_Unit_Priority_List.pdf`
- Export from [docs/RPL_UNIT_PRIORITY_LIST.md](RPL_UNIT_PRIORITY_LIST.md).
- Purpose: helps the assessor understand where your evidence is strongest and where extra explanation may be needed.

7. `07_Optional_Supporting_Artifacts.pdf`
- Include any additional items only if they add value and do not create clutter.
- Examples:
  - specific commit screenshots
  - release screenshots
  - live website screenshots
  - Wayback evidence screenshots
  - test output screenshots
  - deployment screenshots

## Recommended Final Folder Structure

Create one folder containing the exported PDFs in the same order:

- `01_RPL_Cover_Letter.pdf`
- `02_RPL_Evidence_Index.pdf`
- `03_RPL_Maestro_Overview.pdf`
- `04_RPL_Screenshot_Appendix.pdf`
- `05_RPL_Referee_Confirmation.pdf`
- `06_RPL_Unit_Priority_List.pdf`
- `07_Optional_Supporting_Artifacts.pdf`

## What To Check Before Sending

- Confirm your name, dates, and contact details are filled in.
- Confirm Maestro start date wording is accurate: at least Dec 2025, possibly earlier.
- Confirm all screenshots are readable and captions are short and specific.
- Confirm private tokens, email addresses, and sensitive data are redacted.
- Confirm forked repositories are clearly labeled as fork/evaluation evidence.
- Confirm the strongest units are supported first, with weaker units given concise but honest explanations.
